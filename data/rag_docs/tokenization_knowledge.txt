TOPIC: Tokenization
CATEGORY: tokenization

DEFINITION
Tokenization converts text into model-processing units such as words, subwords, characters, or byte-level pieces.

HOW IT WORKS
Modern language models often use subword tokenization so rare words can be decomposed into reusable pieces.

KEY IDEAS
The exact implementation depends on the model family, data type, objective, and operating constraints.
The main design variables are representation, objective function, optimization or search procedure, and evaluation criteria.

COMMON USE CASES
NLP and LLM preprocessing
