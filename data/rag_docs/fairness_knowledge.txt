TOPIC: Fairness in ML
CATEGORY: fairness

DEFINITION
Fairness in ML studies whether model outcomes or errors differ systematically across protected or relevant groups.

HOW IT WORKS
Metrics include demographic parity, equalized odds, equal opportunity, and groupwise calibration, which can conflict under different conditions.

KEY IDEAS
The exact implementation depends on the model family, data type, objective, and operating constraints.
The main design variables are representation, objective function, optimization or search procedure, and evaluation criteria.
