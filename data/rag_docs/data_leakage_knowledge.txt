TOPIC: Data Leakage
CATEGORY: data_leakage

DEFINITION
Data leakage occurs when information unavailable at prediction time influences training or evaluation.

HOW IT WORKS
Leakage can enter through preprocessing, temporal splits, target-derived features, duplicated records, or improper validation.

KEY IDEAS
The exact implementation depends on the model family, data type, objective, and operating constraints.
The main design variables are representation, objective function, optimization or search procedure, and evaluation criteria.
