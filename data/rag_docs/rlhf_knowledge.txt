TOPIC: RLHF
CATEGORY: rlhf

DEFINITION
Reinforcement learning from human feedback uses human preference signals to optimize model behavior after pretraining and supervised adaptation.

HOW IT WORKS
A reward model can learn preference patterns and a reinforcement-learning algorithm can optimize policy behavior.

KEY IDEAS
The exact implementation depends on the model family, data type, objective, and operating constraints.
The main design variables are representation, objective function, optimization or search procedure, and evaluation criteria.

COMMON USE CASES
alignment, helpfulness, preference optimization

ADVANTAGES / STRENGTHS
This approach is useful when its assumptions and inductive biases match the structure of the problem. It can provide a strong baseline, specialized performance, interpretability, efficiency, or scalability depending on the topic.

LIMITATIONS / FAILURE MODES
expensive feedback, reward hacking, optimization instability

COMPARISON
RLHF should be distinguished from closely related approaches in the same problem family. The best choice depends on data size, feature type, dimensionality, latency, accuracy, interpretability, and operational constraints.

EVALUATION / PRACTICAL CHECKS
Choose metrics that match the task and business cost of errors. Validate on a representative holdout or cross-validation design, check leakage, inspect failure cases, and monitor performance after deployment.

INTERVIEW TAKEAWAY
Be prepared to explain what RLHF is, how it works, when to use it, what assumptions it makes, and what trade-offs or failure modes it has.
