TOPIC: Diffusion Models
CATEGORY: diffusion

DEFINITION
Diffusion models generate data by learning to reverse a gradual noise-adding process.

HOW IT WORKS
A common formulation trains a network to predict noise or another parameterization at different noise levels, then samples by iterative denoising.

KEY IDEAS
Training commonly learns to denoise noisy samples at randomly selected diffusion timesteps.
The main design variables are representation, objective function, optimization or search procedure, and evaluation criteria.

COMMON USE CASES
image, audio, and video generation

LIMITATIONS / FAILURE MODES
