TOPIC: Perceptron
CATEGORY: perceptron

DEFINITION
A perceptron computes a weighted sum of inputs and applies a threshold-like decision rule.

HOW IT WORKS
The classical perceptron converges on linearly separable data under suitable conditions.

KEY IDEAS
The exact implementation depends on the model family, data type, objective, and operating constraints.
The main design variables are representation, objective function, optimization or search procedure, and evaluation criteria.

COMMON USE CASES
historical foundation of neural networks, simple linear classification

ADVANTAGES / STRENGTHS
This approach is useful when its assumptions and inductive biases match the structure of the problem. It can provide a strong baseline, specialized performance, interpretability, efficiency, or scalability depending on the topic.

LIMITATIONS / FAILURE MODES
cannot represent nonlinear decision boundaries by itself

COMPARISON
Perceptron should be distinguished from closely related approaches in the same problem family. The best choice depends on data size, feature type, dimensionality, latency, accuracy, interpretability, and operational constraints.
