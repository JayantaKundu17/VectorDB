TOPIC: Differential Privacy
CATEGORY: differential_privacy

DEFINITION
Differential privacy limits how much the output of an analysis can reveal about any individual record.

HOW IT WORKS
Noise or other mechanisms are calibrated using a privacy budget, often represented by epsilon and sometimes delta.

KEY IDEAS
The exact implementation depends on the model family, data type, objective, and operating constraints.
The main design variables are representation, objective function, optimization or search procedure, and evaluation criteria.

COMMON USE CASES
privacy-preserving statistics and ML

ADVANTAGES / STRENGTHS
This approach is useful when its assumptions and inductive biases match the structure of the problem. It can provide a strong baseline, specialized performance, interpretability, efficiency, or scalability depending on the topic.

LIMITATIONS / FAILURE MODES
privacy-utility tradeoff and composition

COMPARISON
Differential Privacy should be distinguished from federated learning. The best choice depends on data size, feature type, dimensionality, latency, accuracy, interpretability, and operational constraints.

EVALUATION / PRACTICAL CHECKS
Choose metrics that match the task and business cost of errors. Validate on a representative holdout or cross-validation design, check leakage, inspect failure cases, and monitor performance after deployment.

INTERVIEW TAKEAWAY
Be prepared to explain what Differential Privacy is, how it works, when to use it, what assumptions it makes, and what trade-offs or failure modes it has.
