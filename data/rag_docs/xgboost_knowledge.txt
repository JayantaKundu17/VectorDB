TOPIC: XGBoost

DEFINITION
XGBoost is a regularized gradient-boosted tree implementation designed for efficient and accurate supervised learning.

HOW IT WORKS
It uses tree boosting with shrinkage, regularization, column or row subsampling, and efficient training strategies.
