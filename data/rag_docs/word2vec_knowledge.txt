TOPIC: Word2Vec
CATEGORY: word2vec

DEFINITION
Word2Vec learns dense word embeddings by predicting words from context or context from a word.

HOW IT WORKS
CBOW predicts a target from nearby words; skip-gram predicts nearby words from a target word.

KEY IDEAS
The exact implementation depends on the model family, data type, objective, and operating constraints.
The main design variables are representation, objective function, optimization or search procedure, and evaluation criteria.

COMMON USE CASES
semantic similarity, classic NLP

ADVANTAGES / STRENGTHS
This approach is useful when its assumptions and inductive biases match the structure of the problem. It can provide a strong baseline, specialized performance, interpretability, efficiency, or scalability depending on the topic.

LIMITATIONS / FAILURE MODES
static embeddings do not change meaning by sentence context

COMPARISON
Word2Vec should be distinguished from closely related approaches in the same problem family. The best choice depends on data size, feature type, dimensionality, latency, accuracy, interpretability, and operational constraints.

EVALUATION / PRACTICAL CHECKS
Choose metrics that match the task and business cost of errors. Validate on a representative holdout or cross-validation design, check leakage, inspect failure cases, and monitor performance after deployment.

INTERVIEW TAKEAWAY
Be prepared to explain what Word2Vec is, how it works, when to use it, what assumptions it makes, and what trade-offs or failure modes it has.
