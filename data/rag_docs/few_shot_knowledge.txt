TOPIC: Few-Shot Learning
CATEGORY: few_shot

DEFINITION
Few-shot prompting gives a model a small number of input-output examples in the prompt.

HOW IT WORKS
The examples demonstrate the desired mapping or format without updating model parameters.

KEY IDEAS
The exact implementation depends on the model family, data type, objective, and operating constraints.
The main design variables are representation, objective function, optimization or search procedure, and evaluation criteria.

COMMON USE CASES
classification, extraction, formatting, task adaptation

ADVANTAGES / STRENGTHS
This approach is useful when its assumptions and inductive biases match the structure of the problem. It can provide a strong baseline, specialized performance, interpretability, efficiency, or scalability depending on the topic.

LIMITATIONS / FAILURE MODES
example quality and prompt length matter

COMPARISON
Few-Shot Learning should be distinguished from closely related approaches in the same problem family. The best choice depends on data size, feature type, dimensionality, latency, accuracy, interpretability, and operational constraints.

EVALUATION / PRACTICAL CHECKS
Choose metrics that match the task and business cost of errors. Validate on a representative holdout or cross-validation design, check leakage, inspect failure cases, and monitor performance after deployment.

INTERVIEW TAKEAWAY
Be prepared to explain what Few-Shot Learning is, how it works, when to use it, what assumptions it makes, and what trade-offs or failure modes it has.
