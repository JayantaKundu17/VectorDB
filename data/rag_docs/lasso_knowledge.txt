TOPIC: Lasso Regression
CATEGORY: lasso

DEFINITION
Lasso regression adds an L1 penalty that can shrink some coefficients exactly to zero.

HOW IT WORKS
The sparsity induced by L1 regularization can act as a form of feature selection.

KEY IDEAS
Objective: squared error + lambda * sum(|w_i|).
The main design variables are representation, objective function, optimization or search procedure, and evaluation criteria.

COMMON USE CASES
high-dimensional regression, sparse models

ADVANTAGES / STRENGTHS
This approach is useful when its assumptions and inductive biases match the structure of the problem. It can provide a strong baseline, specialized performance, interpretability, efficiency, or scalability depending on the topic.

LIMITATIONS / FAILURE MODES
unstable feature selection under highly correlated predictors

COMPARISON
Lasso Regression should be distinguished from Ridge and Elastic Net. The best choice depends on data size, feature type, dimensionality, latency, accuracy, interpretability, and operational constraints.

EVALUATION / PRACTICAL CHECKS
Choose metrics that match the task and business cost of errors. Validate on a representative holdout or cross-validation design, check leakage, inspect failure cases, and monitor performance after deployment.

INTERVIEW TAKEAWAY
Be prepared to explain what Lasso Regression is, how it works, when to use it, what assumptions it makes, and what trade-offs or failure modes it has.
