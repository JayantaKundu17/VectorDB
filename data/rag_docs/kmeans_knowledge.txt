TOPIC: K-Means
CATEGORY: kmeans

DEFINITION
K-means partitions data into k clusters by iteratively assigning points to the nearest centroid and recomputing centroids.

HOW IT WORKS
The standard objective minimizes within-cluster squared distance to centroids.

KEY IDEAS
The exact implementation depends on the model family, data type, objective, and operating constraints.
The main design variables are representation, objective function, optimization or search procedure, and evaluation criteria.

COMMON USE CASES
customer segmentation, vector grouping, compression

ADVANTAGES / STRENGTHS
This approach is useful when its assumptions and inductive biases match the structure of the problem. It can provide a strong baseline, specialized performance, interpretability, efficiency, or scalability depending on the topic.

LIMITATIONS / FAILURE MODES
requires k, sensitive to initialization and scaling, prefers roughly spherical clusters

COMPARISON
K-Means should be distinguished from DBSCAN and hierarchical clustering. The best choice depends on data size, feature type, dimensionality, latency, accuracy, interpretability, and operational constraints.

EVALUATION / PRACTICAL CHECKS
Choose metrics that match the task and business cost of errors. Validate on a representative holdout or cross-validation design, check leakage, inspect failure cases, and monitor performance after deployment.

INTERVIEW TAKEAWAY
Be prepared to explain what K-Means is, how it works, when to use it, what assumptions it makes, and what trade-offs or failure modes it has.
