TOPIC: Attention
CATEGORY: attention

DEFINITION
Attention computes weighted combinations of representations so a model can focus on relevant information.

HOW IT WORKS
The weights are typically derived from compatibility between queries and keys, then applied to values.

KEY IDEAS
Attention is commonly computed as softmax(QK^T / sqrt(d_k))V.
The main design variables are representation, objective function, optimization or search procedure, and evaluation criteria.

COMMON USE CASES
NLP, vision, multimodal learning

ADVANTAGES / STRENGTHS
This approach is useful when its assumptions and inductive biases match the structure of the problem. It can provide a strong baseline, specialized performance, interpretability, efficiency, or scalability depending on the topic.

LIMITATIONS / FAILURE MODES
quadratic cost in sequence length for full self-attention

COMPARISON
Attention should be distinguished from closely related approaches in the same problem family. The best choice depends on data size, feature type, dimensionality, latency, accuracy, interpretability, and operational constraints.

EVALUATION / PRACTICAL CHECKS
Choose metrics that match the task and business cost of errors. Validate on a representative holdout or cross-validation design, check leakage, inspect failure cases, and monitor performance after deployment.

INTERVIEW TAKEAWAY
Be prepared to explain what Attention is, how it works, when to use it, what assumptions it makes, and what trade-offs or failure modes it has.
