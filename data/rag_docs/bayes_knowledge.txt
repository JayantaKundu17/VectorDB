TOPIC: Bayes Theorem
CATEGORY: bayes

DEFINITION
Bayes theorem updates a prior belief using evidence to produce a posterior belief.

HOW IT WORKS
P(A|B) = P(B|A)P(A) / P(B). Bayesian reasoning separates prior assumptions, likelihood, and posterior belief.

KEY IDEAS
