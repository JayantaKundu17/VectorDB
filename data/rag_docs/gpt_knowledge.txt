TOPIC: GPT
CATEGORY: gpt

DEFINITION
GPT-style models are decoder-only transformers trained autoregressively to predict the next token.

HOW IT WORKS
Given a token sequence, the model estimates a probability distribution over the next token and generates by repeated decoding.

KEY IDEAS
The exact implementation depends on the model family, data type, objective, and operating constraints.
The main design variables are representation, objective function, optimization or search procedure, and evaluation criteria.

COMMON USE CASES
text generation, coding, assistants

ADVANTAGES / STRENGTHS
This approach is useful when its assumptions and inductive biases match the structure of the problem. It can provide a strong baseline, specialized performance, interpretability, efficiency, or scalability depending on the topic.

LIMITATIONS / FAILURE MODES
next-token likelihood does not guarantee factuality or instruction following

COMPARISON
GPT should be distinguished from BERT. The best choice depends on data size, feature type, dimensionality, latency, accuracy, interpretability, and operational constraints.

EVALUATION / PRACTICAL CHECKS
Choose metrics that match the task and business cost of errors. Validate on a representative holdout or cross-validation design, check leakage, inspect failure cases, and monitor performance after deployment.

INTERVIEW TAKEAWAY
Be prepared to explain what GPT is, how it works, when to use it, what assumptions it makes, and what trade-offs or failure modes it has.
