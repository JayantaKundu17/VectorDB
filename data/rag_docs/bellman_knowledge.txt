TOPIC: Bellman Equation
CATEGORY: bellman

DEFINITION
The Bellman equations express a value function recursively in terms of immediate reward and expected future value.

HOW IT WORKS
For a policy, V(s)=E[r + gamma V(s')] in the standard discounted formulation. Optimal Bellman equations replace policy expectations with maximizing actions.

KEY IDEAS
Policy-value form: V(s) = E[r + gamma V(s') | s].
The main design variables are representation, objective function, optimization or search procedure, and evaluation criteria.

COMMON USE CASES
dynamic programming and RL
