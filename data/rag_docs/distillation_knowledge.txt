TOPIC: Knowledge Distillation
CATEGORY: distillation

DEFINITION
Knowledge distillation trains a smaller student model to reproduce useful behavior from a larger teacher model.

HOW IT WORKS
The student can learn from teacher probabilities or intermediate representations in addition to hard labels.

KEY IDEAS
The exact implementation depends on the model family, data type, objective, and operating constraints.
The main design variables are representation, objective function, optimization or search procedure, and evaluation criteria.

COMMON USE CASES
model compression and deployment

ADVANTAGES / STRENGTHS
This approach is useful when its assumptions and inductive biases match the structure of the problem. It can provide a strong baseline, specialized performance, interpretability, efficiency, or scalability depending on the topic.

LIMITATIONS / FAILURE MODES
teacher errors and task mismatch can transfer

COMPARISON
Knowledge Distillation should be distinguished from closely related approaches in the same problem family. The best choice depends on data size, feature type, dimensionality, latency, accuracy, interpretability, and operational constraints.

EVALUATION / PRACTICAL CHECKS
Choose metrics that match the task and business cost of errors. Validate on a representative holdout or cross-validation design, check leakage, inspect failure cases, and monitor performance after deployment.

INTERVIEW TAKEAWAY
Be prepared to explain what Knowledge Distillation is, how it works, when to use it, what assumptions it makes, and what trade-offs or failure modes it has.
