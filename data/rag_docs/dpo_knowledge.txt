TOPIC: DPO
CATEGORY: dpo

DEFINITION
Direct Preference Optimization trains a policy directly from preference pairs without separately running an online reinforcement-learning loop in the standard formulation.

HOW IT WORKS
The objective increases the relative likelihood of preferred responses compared with rejected responses under a reference model.

KEY IDEAS
The exact implementation depends on the model family, data type, objective, and operating constraints.
The main design variables are representation, objective function, optimization or search procedure, and evaluation criteria.

COMMON USE CASES
LLM alignment and preference tuning

ADVANTAGES / STRENGTHS
This approach is useful when its assumptions and inductive biases match the structure of the problem. It can provide a strong baseline, specialized performance, interpretability, efficiency, or scalability depending on the topic.

LIMITATIONS / FAILURE MODES
quality of preference data and reference-model choices matter

COMPARISON
DPO should be distinguished from closely related approaches in the same problem family. The best choice depends on data size, feature type, dimensionality, latency, accuracy, interpretability, and operational constraints.

EVALUATION / PRACTICAL CHECKS
Choose metrics that match the task and business cost of errors. Validate on a representative holdout or cross-validation design, check leakage, inspect failure cases, and monitor performance after deployment.

INTERVIEW TAKEAWAY
Be prepared to explain what DPO is, how it works, when to use it, what assumptions it makes, and what trade-offs or failure modes it has.
