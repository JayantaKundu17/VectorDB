TOPIC: AI System Design
CATEGORY: ai_system_design

DEFINITION
AI system design combines model choice with data pipelines, retrieval, serving, security, evaluation, and operations.

HOW IT WORKS
Good designs define requirements, latency and cost targets, failure modes, observability, and fallback strategies before choosing components.

KEY IDEAS
The exact implementation depends on the model family, data type, objective, and operating constraints.
The main design variables are representation, objective function, optimization or search procedure, and evaluation criteria.

COMMON USE CASES
production AI applications

ADVANTAGES / STRENGTHS
This approach is useful when its assumptions and inductive biases match the structure of the problem. It can provide a strong baseline, specialized performance, interpretability, efficiency, or scalability depending on the topic.

LIMITATIONS / FAILURE MODES
a strong model cannot compensate for poor system architecture

COMPARISON
AI System Design should be distinguished from closely related approaches in the same problem family. The best choice depends on data size, feature type, dimensionality, latency, accuracy, interpretability, and operational constraints.

EVALUATION / PRACTICAL CHECKS
Choose metrics that match the task and business cost of errors. Validate on a representative holdout or cross-validation design, check leakage, inspect failure cases, and monitor performance after deployment.

INTERVIEW TAKEAWAY
Be prepared to explain what AI System Design is, how it works, when to use it, what assumptions it makes, and what trade-offs or failure modes it has.
