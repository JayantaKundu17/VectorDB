TOPIC: Symbolic AI
CATEGORY: symbolic_ai

DEFINITION
Symbolic AI represents knowledge with explicit symbols, rules, logic, or structured search.

HOW IT WORKS
Knowledge is encoded explicitly rather than inferred only from continuous parameters. Rule engines, planning, theorem proving, and expert systems are classic examples.

KEY IDEAS
The exact implementation depends on the model family, data type, objective, and operating constraints.
The main design variables are representation, objective function, optimization or search procedure, and evaluation criteria.

COMMON USE CASES
expert systems, planning, logic, constrained reasoning

ADVANTAGES / STRENGTHS
This approach is useful when its assumptions and inductive biases match the structure of the problem. It can provide a strong baseline, specialized performance, interpretability, efficiency, or scalability depending on the topic.

LIMITATIONS / FAILURE MODES
brittleness, knowledge engineering cost, weak handling of noisy unstructured data

COMPARISON
Symbolic AI should be distinguished from closely related approaches in the same problem family. The best choice depends on data size, feature type, dimensionality, latency, accuracy, interpretability, and operational constraints.

EVALUATION / PRACTICAL CHECKS
Choose metrics that match the task and business cost of errors. Validate on a representative holdout or cross-validation design, check leakage, inspect failure cases, and monitor performance after deployment.

INTERVIEW TAKEAWAY
Be prepared to explain what Symbolic AI is, how it works, when to use it, what assumptions it makes, and what trade-offs or failure modes it has.
