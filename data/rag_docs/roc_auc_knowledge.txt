TOPIC: ROC AUC
CATEGORY: roc_auc

DEFINITION
ROC AUC summarizes ranking performance across classification thresholds using true-positive and false-positive rates.

HOW IT WORKS
It can be interpreted as the probability that a randomly chosen positive receives a higher score than a randomly chosen negative under common conditions.

KEY IDEAS
AUC is the area under the ROC curve across thresholds.
The main design variables are representation, objective function, optimization or search procedure, and evaluation criteria.

COMMON USE CASES
binary classification

ADVANTAGES / STRENGTHS
This approach is useful when its assumptions and inductive biases match the structure of the problem. It can provide a strong baseline, specialized performance, interpretability, efficiency, or scalability depending on the topic.

LIMITATIONS / FAILURE MODES
can look strong even when precision at the operating point is poor under severe imbalance

COMPARISON
ROC AUC should be distinguished from closely related approaches in the same problem family. The best choice depends on data size, feature type, dimensionality, latency, accuracy, interpretability, and operational constraints.

EVALUATION / PRACTICAL CHECKS
Choose metrics that match the task and business cost of errors. Validate on a representative holdout or cross-validation design, check leakage, inspect failure cases, and monitor performance after deployment.

INTERVIEW TAKEAWAY
Be prepared to explain what ROC AUC is, how it works, when to use it, what assumptions it makes, and what trade-offs or failure modes it has.
