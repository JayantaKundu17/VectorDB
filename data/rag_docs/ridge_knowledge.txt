TOPIC: Ridge Regression
CATEGORY: ridge

DEFINITION
Ridge regression adds an L2 penalty to the linear-regression objective to shrink coefficients toward zero.

HOW IT WORKS
The penalty can reduce variance and stabilize estimates when features are correlated.

KEY IDEAS
Objective: squared error + lambda * sum(w_i^2).
The main design variables are representation, objective function, optimization or search procedure, and evaluation criteria.

COMMON USE CASES
regularized regression, correlated predictors

ADVANTAGES / STRENGTHS
This approach is useful when its assumptions and inductive biases match the structure of the problem. It can provide a strong baseline, specialized performance, interpretability, efficiency, or scalability depending on the topic.

LIMITATIONS / FAILURE MODES
regularization strength must be tuned and coefficients remain nonzero

COMPARISON
Ridge Regression should be distinguished from Lasso and Elastic Net. The best choice depends on data size, feature type, dimensionality, latency, accuracy, interpretability, and operational constraints.

EVALUATION / PRACTICAL CHECKS
Choose metrics that match the task and business cost of errors. Validate on a representative holdout or cross-validation design, check leakage, inspect failure cases, and monitor performance after deployment.

INTERVIEW TAKEAWAY
Be prepared to explain what Ridge Regression is, how it works, when to use it, what assumptions it makes, and what trade-offs or failure modes it has.
