TOPIC: Deep Learning
CATEGORY: dl

DEFINITION
Deep learning uses neural networks with multiple learned layers to transform inputs into useful representations and predictions.

HOW IT WORKS
Deep networks learn hierarchical representations using forward passes, differentiable losses, backpropagation, and optimization.

KEY IDEAS
The exact implementation depends on the model family, data type, objective, and operating constraints.
The main design variables are representation, objective function, optimization or search procedure, and evaluation criteria.

COMMON USE CASES
vision, speech, NLP, multimodal learning, generative AI

ADVANTAGES / STRENGTHS
This approach is useful when its assumptions and inductive biases match the structure of the problem. It can provide a strong baseline, specialized performance, interpretability, efficiency, or scalability depending on the topic.

LIMITATIONS / FAILURE MODES
large compute requirements, data requirements, instability, limited interpretability

COMPARISON
Deep Learning should be distinguished from classical ML. The best choice depends on data size, feature type, dimensionality, latency, accuracy, interpretability, and operational constraints.

EVALUATION / PRACTICAL CHECKS
Choose metrics that match the task and business cost of errors. Validate on a representative holdout or cross-validation design, check leakage, inspect failure cases, and monitor performance after deployment.

INTERVIEW TAKEAWAY
Be prepared to explain what Deep Learning is, how it works, when to use it, what assumptions it makes, and what trade-offs or failure modes it has.
