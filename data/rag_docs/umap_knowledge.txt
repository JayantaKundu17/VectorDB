TOPIC: UMAP
CATEGORY: umap

DEFINITION
UMAP is a manifold-learning method that builds a graph of local relationships and optimizes a lower-dimensional representation.

HOW IT WORKS
It is often faster than t-SNE on larger datasets and can preserve both local and some global structure depending on settings.
