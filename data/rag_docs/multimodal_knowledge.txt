TOPIC: Multimodal AI
CATEGORY: multimodal

DEFINITION
Multimodal AI learns or reasons across multiple data types such as text, images, audio, video, and structured data.

HOW IT WORKS
Systems may use shared embeddings, cross-attention, modality-specific encoders, or unified token spaces.

KEY IDEAS
The exact implementation depends on the model family, data type, objective, and operating constraints.
The main design variables are representation, objective function, optimization or search procedure, and evaluation criteria.

COMMON USE CASES
vision-language models, assistants, search

ADVANTAGES / STRENGTHS
This approach is useful when its assumptions and inductive biases match the structure of the problem. It can provide a strong baseline, specialized performance, interpretability, efficiency, or scalability depending on the topic.

LIMITATIONS / FAILURE MODES
alignment across modalities, data scale, and evaluation complexity

COMPARISON
Multimodal AI should be distinguished from closely related approaches in the same problem family. The best choice depends on data size, feature type, dimensionality, latency, accuracy, interpretability, and operational constraints.

EVALUATION / PRACTICAL CHECKS
Choose metrics that match the task and business cost of errors. Validate on a representative holdout or cross-validation design, check leakage, inspect failure cases, and monitor performance after deployment.

INTERVIEW TAKEAWAY
Be prepared to explain what Multimodal AI is, how it works, when to use it, what assumptions it makes, and what trade-offs or failure modes it has.
