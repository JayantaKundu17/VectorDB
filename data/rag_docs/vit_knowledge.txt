TOPIC: Vision Transformers
CATEGORY: vit

DEFINITION
Vision Transformers apply transformer-style self-attention to sequences of image patches or visual tokens.

HOW IT WORKS
An image can be split into patches, projected into embeddings, augmented with positional information, and processed by attention blocks.

KEY IDEAS
The exact implementation depends on the model family, data type, objective, and operating constraints.
The main design variables are representation, objective function, optimization or search procedure, and evaluation criteria.
