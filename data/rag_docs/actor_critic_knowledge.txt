TOPIC: Actor-Critic
CATEGORY: actor_critic

DEFINITION
Actor-critic methods combine a policy model (actor) with a value estimator (critic).

HOW IT WORKS
The critic helps reduce variance in policy-gradient updates by estimating expected return or advantage.

KEY IDEAS
The exact implementation depends on the model family, data type, objective, and operating constraints.
The main design variables are representation, objective function, optimization or search procedure, and evaluation criteria.

COMMON USE CASES
general RL and continuous control

ADVANTAGES / STRENGTHS
This approach is useful when its assumptions and inductive biases match the structure of the problem. It can provide a strong baseline, specialized performance, interpretability, efficiency, or scalability depending on the topic.

LIMITATIONS / FAILURE MODES
critic bias and instability

COMPARISON
Actor-Critic should be distinguished from closely related approaches in the same problem family. The best choice depends on data size, feature type, dimensionality, latency, accuracy, interpretability, and operational constraints.

EVALUATION / PRACTICAL CHECKS
Choose metrics that match the task and business cost of errors. Validate on a representative holdout or cross-validation design, check leakage, inspect failure cases, and monitor performance after deployment.

INTERVIEW TAKEAWAY
Be prepared to explain what Actor-Critic is, how it works, when to use it, what assumptions it makes, and what trade-offs or failure modes it has.
