TOPIC: Calibration
CATEGORY: calibration

DEFINITION
A model is calibrated when predicted probabilities correspond to observed frequencies over groups of similarly scored examples.

HOW IT WORKS
Reliability diagrams and calibration-error measures assess this behavior. Platt scaling and isotonic regression can recalibrate scores.

KEY IDEAS
The exact implementation depends on the model family, data type, objective, and operating constraints.
The main design variables are representation, objective function, optimization or search procedure, and evaluation criteria.
