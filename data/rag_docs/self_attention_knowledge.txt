TOPIC: Self-Attention
CATEGORY: self_attention

DEFINITION
Self-attention lets each token compute a weighted view of other tokens in the same sequence.

HOW IT WORKS
Queries, keys, and values are projected from the token representations. Attention weights come from query-key compatibility and are applied to values.

KEY IDEAS
Attention is commonly computed as softmax(QK^T / sqrt(d_k))V.
The main design variables are representation, objective function, optimization or search procedure, and evaluation criteria.

COMMON USE CASES
transformers and contextual representation learning

ADVANTAGES / STRENGTHS
This approach is useful when its assumptions and inductive biases match the structure of the problem. It can provide a strong baseline, specialized performance, interpretability, efficiency, or scalability depending on the topic.

LIMITATIONS / FAILURE MODES
full attention can be expensive for long sequences

COMPARISON
Self-Attention should be distinguished from closely related approaches in the same problem family. The best choice depends on data size, feature type, dimensionality, latency, accuracy, interpretability, and operational constraints.

EVALUATION / PRACTICAL CHECKS
Choose metrics that match the task and business cost of errors. Validate on a representative holdout or cross-validation design, check leakage, inspect failure cases, and monitor performance after deployment.

INTERVIEW TAKEAWAY
Be prepared to explain what Self-Attention is, how it works, when to use it, what assumptions it makes, and what trade-offs or failure modes it has.
