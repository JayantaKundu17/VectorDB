TOPIC: Categorical Encoding
CATEGORY: encoding

DEFINITION
Categorical encoding converts discrete labels into numerical representations suitable for a model.

HOW IT WORKS
One-hot encoding is simple for low-cardinality categories. Ordinal or target encoding can be useful with care.

KEY IDEAS
The exact implementation depends on the model family, data type, objective, and operating constraints.
The main design variables are representation, objective function, optimization or search procedure, and evaluation criteria.

COMMON USE CASES
tabular ML, recommendation systems

ADVANTAGES / STRENGTHS
This approach is useful when its assumptions and inductive biases match the structure of the problem. It can provide a strong baseline, specialized performance, interpretability, efficiency, or scalability depending on the topic.

LIMITATIONS / FAILURE MODES
unseen categories, high cardinality, target leakage

COMPARISON
Categorical Encoding should be distinguished from closely related approaches in the same problem family. The best choice depends on data size, feature type, dimensionality, latency, accuracy, interpretability, and operational constraints.

EVALUATION / PRACTICAL CHECKS
Choose metrics that match the task and business cost of errors. Validate on a representative holdout or cross-validation design, check leakage, inspect failure cases, and monitor performance after deployment.

INTERVIEW TAKEAWAY
Be prepared to explain what Categorical Encoding is, how it works, when to use it, what assumptions it makes, and what trade-offs or failure modes it has.
