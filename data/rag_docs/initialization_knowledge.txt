TOPIC: Weight Initialization
CATEGORY: initialization

DEFINITION
Weight initialization chooses starting parameters so signal and gradients remain usable during training.

HOW IT WORKS
Xavier/Glorot is common for certain activations, while He initialization is designed for ReLU-like networks.

KEY IDEAS
The exact implementation depends on the model family, data type, objective, and operating constraints.
The main design variables are representation, objective function, optimization or search procedure, and evaluation criteria.

COMMON USE CASES
deep neural network training
